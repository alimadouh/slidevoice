B7oothKw · narration, automatically placed

Put your voice on every slide.

Drop in your slides, your script, and your recordings. B7oothKw listens to each clip, works out which slide it belongs to, and gives you back a narrated PowerPoint. Everything runs in your browser — your files never leave your device.

1

PowerPoint

Your .pptx deck. Required.

2

Script (optional)

.docx / .txt with “Slide N” headings. Improves matching.

3

Recordings

One clip per slide — mp3, m4a, wav, ogg… Drop them all at once.
