Support & FAQ

What does B7oothKw do?

It rewrites AI-sounding text so it reads naturally. The same account also humanizes a whole PowerPoint deck or Word document, orders a real Turnitin check on a file you upload, and turns a deck into a narrated presentation with the Slide Narrator.

Do you store my text or my files?

Text you paste into the humanizer is processed in memory and discarded after the request — we don't keep it. A PowerPoint or Word file is held only long enough to build your download, then dropped 15 minutes after it's ready. A document you upload for a paid Turnitin scan has to be stored so we can run the scan and send you the reports; those files are deleted seven days after the order is completed.

Do I need to verify my email to sign up?

Yes — when you register with an email and password we send a 6-digit code to confirm it's really you. Signing in with Google skips this step, since Google already verified your email.

Which browsers are supported?

Any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, and mobile browsers. Internet Explorer is not supported.

How do I get help or contact support?

Use the Live Support chat on this page — type your message on the right and our team will get back to you, usually within a few hours. You can also WhatsApp us on 55495757.

How many words do I get for free?

150 words, once. They come with your account and they don't reset each day — once they're gone, a month gives you 10,000 more.

Is there a limit on one humanize?

Yes — 500 words per run, whichever plan you're on. Longer text goes through in a few runs, or upload it as a Word document instead.

What do the green, yellow, and red colours mean?

After humanizing, each sentence is colour-coded by how human it reads: green is human, yellow is borderline, and red still looks AI.

What is “Go Green”?

It re-rewrites only the sentences still flagged yellow or red, until they read as human, they stop improving, or a safe limit is reached. Go Green never uses your words — it's free on every plan.

I humanized my text, but it's still flagged as AI. Why?

Some sentences are tougher than others. Run Go Green to re-pass the ones still highlighted, or humanize again to explore different phrasings. No humanizer can guarantee a particular detector score — detectors change their models regularly, and results vary by text.

Does it change my facts, names, or numbers?

It aims to keep your meaning. It will not introduce new names or numbers — if a sentence can't be rewritten faithfully, it's left as you wrote it.

Is the PowerPoint humanizer free?

Yes — up to 8 decks a day, and it doesn't touch your word balance. Upload a .pptx, and you get the same deck back with the text rewritten.

Who can use the Word humanizer?

It needs a month, because it rewrites a whole document in one go. Members get 5 documents a day, up to 10,000 words per file, and the words come out of your monthly balance.

Does my formatting survive?

Yes. You get your own file back — slides, headings, tables, images and layout stay where they were. Only the wording changes.

How long does a file take?

It depends on the length: a short deck is about a minute, a long Word document can take several. Keep the tab open while it runs — the download appears on the page as soon as it's ready.

Is this a real Turnitin report?

Yes. Your file is run on real Turnitin by our team, and you get the full report back — both the AI and similarity percentages, as downloadable PDFs that keep your formatting and figures.

What does a scan cost, and how long does it take?

3.000 KWD per scan, with results back within 6 hours. They appear under My scans on the Turnitin page, and you can message the team about an order from there.

Does a scan expire?

No. A scan you've bought sits on your account until you use it — buy one now, submit whenever.

Do I need a membership to buy a scan?

No. Scans are sold one at a time and work on any account, with or without a month.

Which files can I submit?

.doc, .docx, .ppt, .pptx and .pdf.

What does a month include?

9.500 KWD for 30 days and 10,000 words, with Go Green free on top and the Word humanizer unlocked. See the plans page for the full list.

Does it renew automatically?

No. It's a single payment — your card isn't saved and is never charged again. When the month ends, you decide whether to buy another.

What if I run out before the month is over?

Buying again stacks. The new 10,000 words are added to whatever is left, and the 30 days are added to your current end date — nothing is lost.

How do I pay?

KNET, Visa, Mastercard or Apple Pay, through MyFatoorah. Prices are in Kuwaiti dinar, and the payment page opens straight from the plans page.

I have a question about a payment or a refund.

Message us in the chat on this page, or WhatsApp 55495757 — we answer about payments, scans and refunds.
