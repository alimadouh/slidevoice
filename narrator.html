How it works

  1. 1Upload your deck.pptx, the slides you presented
  2. 2Add the script.docx, .txt or .md — what you said
  3. 3Drop in your recordingsOne clip per slide, any order
  4. 4Check the matchesThen download the narrated deck

What you can expect

  • Each recording is matched to its slide by what it says, not its filename.
  • You can listen and re-assign any clip before generating.
  • The audio is embedded in the file — it plays offline, no internet needed.
  • Your slides, fonts and layout are never changed.
