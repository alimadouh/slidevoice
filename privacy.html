Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This policy explains what B7oothKw ("we", "us") collects when you use b7ooth-ai.com, why, who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do about it. We are the controller of that data. Reach us through the Support page or WhatsApp us on 55495757.

The short version. Text you paste is processed and then thrown away. Word and PowerPoint files are dropped 15 minutes after your download is ready. Files you upload for a Turnitin scan are deleted 7 days after the order completes. We never see your card details. We do not sell your data.

What we collect, and why

  • Account data — your email and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), plus the 6-digit code that verifies your address. Sign in with Google and we receive your email and name from Google. Why: to create your account, apply your membership, and contact you about it. Basis: our contract with you.
  • Usage data — words used, files humanized and scans ordered, so your membership pool and daily file limits are applied fairly, plus login records: IP address, approximate country, device and time. Why: to run your membership and to spot abuse and shared accounts. Basis: contract, and our interest in keeping the Service secure.
  • What you submit — text you paste, decks and documents you humanize, and files you upload for a Turnitin scan. Why: only to produce the result you asked for. Basis: our contract with you.
  • Payment data — handled entirely by MyFatoorah. We receive only the invoice and transaction identifiers, the amount, the status, and the email used to pay. Why: to activate your membership and stop it when you cancel. Basis: contract, and our legal duty to keep transaction records.
  • Support messages — what you send us in Live Support or on WhatsApp, and our replies. Basis: our interest in answering you properly.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers.

How long we keep it

  • Text you paste — processed in memory to produce your result, then discarded. We do not save it.
  • Word and PowerPoint files — held only long enough to build your download, then dropped 15 minutes after it is ready.
  • Turnitin uploads and their reports — stored so we can run the scan and send you the reports, reachable by staff only to process your order, and deleted 7 days after the order completes.
  • Account data — while your account exists; removed when you close it or ask us to.
  • Login and security records — a limited period, so you and we can review recent activity.
  • Word and scan balances — while your account is active.
  • Billing records — as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after you close your account. This is the one thing deletion cannot remove.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember preferences such as your theme. These are necessary for the Service to work — clearing them signs you out. At checkout, our payment provider also sets cookies needed to process the payment securely.

Who else processes your data

  • MyFatoorah — card payments. You enter your card on their own secure page, so those details go to them directly and never through us.
  • Google (Sign-In) — only if you choose it; we receive your email and name to identify your account.
  • Turnitin — the file you upload for a scan is submitted for checking, which is the whole point of that order.
  • Email delivery provider — verification codes, password resets and account notices.
  • Cloudflare — delivers and protects the Service, processing connection data such as your IP address to route and secure traffic.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, where it is needed to bring or defend a legal claim, or to investigate abuse of the Service. If the Service is ever transferred to another owner, your data may transfer with it, subject to this policy.

International transfers

We operate from Kuwait and the providers above operate internationally, so your data may be processed outside your own country. For users in the EEA or UK, those providers rely on recognised transfer mechanisms such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, traffic to the Service is encrypted in transit, and stored files are reachable only by staff who need them for your order. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach ever affects your personal data we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country if that is higher, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Tell us if you believe a child has created an account and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting what we did before.

To use them, contact us from the account you are asking about and say which right you want. That is usually enough to verify you; if it is not, we may ask one more question first. We aim to answer within 30 days, free of charge. Deleting your account removes your account data and usage records; the billing records tax law obliges us to keep are the exception.

Changes

We may update this policy as the Service changes. The date above always shows the current version, and we will announce material changes in the Service or by email.

Contact
